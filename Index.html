I'm Akash — a student who has been asking "why?" since before I could spell it. As a kid I would stare at the night sky and wonder what was holding it all together. That curiosity never left. It just got bigger.

Today I explore astronomy to understand where we are, physics to understand how it all works, chemistry to see what everything is made of, biology to understand life, and artificial intelligence to build a piece of that future myself.
